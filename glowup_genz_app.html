Today's checklist

This is a flexible template, not medical advice. Rest when unwell or sore. No crash diets, forced workouts, skin bleaching, harsh DIY face treatments, or supplements are needed. If you are still growing, prioritise regular meals, sleep and guidance from a trusted adult or health professional when needed.

Simple skin & self-care

Gentle basics beat harsh hacks. Patch-test new products and stop anything that irritates your skin.

☀️ Morning routine

Reapply sunscreen as directed, especially outdoors. A hat and shade help too.

🌙 Evening routine

Avoid lemon juice, baking soda, toothpaste, rough scrubs and unknown mixtures on your face. They can irritate skin.

🥗 Everyday nourishment

Eat enough and aim for variety: grains, lentils/beans, dairy or fortified alternatives, vegetables, fruit, nuts and seeds as suitable. No single food or homemade mask transforms your face.

🛌 Recovery & confidence

Keep a regular sleep schedule, enjoy movement, practise good hygiene and choose clothes you feel comfortable in. Skin concerns that persist or hurt are best discussed with a healthcare professional.
